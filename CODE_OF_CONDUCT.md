# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- communicate constructively and assume good intent;
- focus criticism on code, design, and evidence rather than people;
- accept feedback professionally;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing credentials, personal data, or authenticated FL.ru content;
- use the project responsibly and never propose bypassing CAPTCHA, access controls, or platform restrictions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, doxxing, sexualized content, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns privately using the contact method documented in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors whose behavior violates this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and other spaces where a person represents the project.
